Paneer in a rich, mildly sweet tomato and cashew gravy.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity | Unit |
|------------|----------|------|
| Paneer | 200 | g |
| Onion | 100 | g |
| Tomato | 150 | g |
| Cashew | 30 | g |
| Ginger | 10 | g |
| Garlic | 10 | g |
| Green Chilli | 1 | pcs |
| Coriander Leaf | 10 | g |
| Fresh Cream | 50 | ml |

## Recipe

1. Simmer onion, tomato, cashew, ginger, garlic and green chilli till soft, then blend smooth.
2. Strain the puree back into the pan and cook with 20 g butter and ground spices.
3. Add a splash of water and simmer to a glossy gravy.
4. Fold in the paneer cubes and warm through.
5. Finish with the cream, crushed kasuri methi and coriander.
