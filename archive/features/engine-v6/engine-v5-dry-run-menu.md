# Engine v5 dry-run menu: 10 weeks from 2026-08-31

A read-only dry run of the engine v5 spec (`features/engine-v5.md`), generated 2026-08-25 by a
prototype simulation, self-feeding on the household as-eaten record (5 seed weeks plus 8 served
weeks with swaps applied, from prod, read-only). Nothing was written to production; this is not a
production menu.

Known approximations of the prototype against the spec: international lunches sit on fixed days
(Tuesday, Friday) and exploration on Wednesday/Monday rather than recency-placed; the exploration
affinity score uses two of its three signals (no derived macros loaded); the §9 item-cap safety net
and the picker are not exercised. Labels in parentheses mark why a dish landed.

Dry-run measurements against the §10 gate thresholds: week-over-week dish-set Jaccard 0.22
(band 0.20 to 0.35; household baseline 0.263); lunch sizes 3 items on 32 of 60 days, 4 items on 6
(all protein-floor days); 14 distinct breakfast items; dessert on 10 of 10 Saturdays; dal-family
dish in 9 of 10 weeks; exploration sustained at 1 to 2 per week, 11 distinct dishes; zero days over
the 120-minute prep ceiling. Known soft spot: mutton never surfaces in 10 weeks (its record count
is too low to compete; a favorites entry or a content decision, not an engine rule, is the lever).
