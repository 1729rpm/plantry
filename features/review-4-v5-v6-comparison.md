# Reviewer report: v5 dry run vs v6 dry run, resolution audit and fresh comparison

Basis: v6 dry run (60 days, 50 weekday breakfasts, 60 lunches, 60 fruit entries) measured on each of review-1's axes with review-1's counting method, against the v5 dry run (same shape) and the household's 8 as-eaten weeks (44 days, 36 breakfasts, 44 lunches). All counts here were re-tabulated from the raw menu text, not taken from either file's preamble. Spot-checks of prior numbers: review-1's v5 chicken count (23), v5 plain-roti Monday lock (10/10), and household fish tikka (7) and plain roti (17/44) all verified exactly; two review-1 figures recount slightly differently from the raw text (the v5 three-fruit share is 42/60, 70 percent, not 47/60, and household egg breakfasts are 17/36, not 18/36); the v6 preamble's 16/60 four-item count and 7 standalone boiled-egg breakfasts verified exactly. No direction of any finding changes.

**Verdict.** v6 resolves the structural complaints that defined v5: the day-of-week templates are gone, Saturday is a real rotation, rotis and dal and fish tikka sit at household rates, carb internationals get their protein, and lunch-main uniqueness now exceeds the household's own. Scoreboard: 7 resolved, 2 improved, 3 persist, 1 worse. What persists is content-shaped (Winter fruit pool, paneer pressure from exploration, salad over-supply), and the one axis that got worse (international rate) flipped from over-serving to a warm-up under-serve. The fresh comparison, however, finds real regressions review-1's axes do not cover: v6 plates are bigger than both v5 and the household (27 percent four-item lunches vs 7 percent household), the share of lunch days carrying animal protein or egg jumps to 0.73 vs the household's 0.57 (v5 matched at 0.60), egg-led lunches run at 0.8 per week vs the household's 0.125, and week-to-week continuity drops below both the band and v5. The protein floor and the exploration channel, the two mechanisms doing the most good elsewhere, are also the drivers of every steady regression.

## Part 1: finding-by-finding resolution audit

1. **Rigid day-of-week breakfast template. RESOLVED.** v5: roughly 47 of 50 breakfasts followed a fixed weekday template (Tuesday avocado toast 10/10, Wednesday besan chilla 9/10, Friday garlic chutney 10/10, Monday stuffed paratha 9/10). v6: no non-Thursday slot exceeds 3/10 (Monday avocado toast 3/10, Friday avocado toast 3/10, Wednesday's modal item 2/10); avocado toast's 10 servings spread over four weekdays. Household: no repeating day assignment (modal slots 3/6 to 4/7). The one surviving lock is Thursday eggs (v6 10/10 egg-based, boiled eggs 8/10), which matches the household's own 7/8 Thursday egg anchor; note that this same lock fails v6's own §11 gate 4 as written.

2. **Three-fruit collapse. PERSISTS.** v5: banana, papaya, pomegranate on 42/60 fruit days (70 percent), with weeks 6 to 10 at 30/30 and mango at 0.5/week. v6: identically 42/60 (70 percent), weeks 6 to 10 at 30/30, mango 5/60 (0.5/week, zero in Winter), top fruit pomegranate 15/60. Household: 17/44 (39 percent), 9 fruits throughout, mango top at 1.4/week. The Monsoon half of v6 runs 9 distinct fruits with no repeats, same as v5; the Winter half collapses to exactly 3 because the eligible eaten Winter pool is 3 dishes, a library-and-record constraint the §14.4 content task owns, not a mechanism v6 changed.

3. **International lunches: locked to Tuesday and Friday, over-served. WORSE on rate, resolved on placement.** v5: 2.0/week, 20/20 on Tuesday and Friday. v6: 4 weekday internationals in 10 weeks (Spanish omelette, mushroom risotto, shakshuka, Vietnamese noodle salad) plus one Saturday treat (Singapore noodles), 0.4 to 0.5/week, landing on Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, and a Saturday with no fixed day. Household: about 1.4/week, floating days. Absolute deviation grew (v5 was 0.6/week over, v6 is 0.9/week under), so the rate axis is worse at this horizon; the day-lock axis is fully resolved. The under-serve is mechanically a warm-up effect (once-eaten internationals carry a rate of 1/13 and need roughly 13 weeks of accrual), so the real gate's weeks 20 to 60 are the test.

4. **Saturday pool of four in a strict cycle. RESOLVED.** v5: 10 Saturdays from 4 mains in a 4-week cycle; dessert a strict 3-item cycle. v6: 8 distinct treats across 10 Saturdays (khichdi, chole bhature, pav bhaji, rajma chawal, Singapore noodles, curd rice, aloo puri, veg pulao; the first 8 Saturdays are all distinct, and the two repeats sit 7 Saturdays apart), with 5 distinct desserts including shrikhand and suji halwa that v5 never served. Household: 8/8 distinct mains, 5 distinct desserts.

5. **Specialty rotis over, plain roti under. RESOLVED.** Missi roti: v5 1.0/week (Wednesday-locked), v6 0.3/week on assorted days, household 0.25/week. Beetroot roti: v5 1.0/week (Thursday-locked), v6 0.4/week, household 0.375/week. Plain roti: v5 1.0/week (Monday-locked 10/10), v6 2.8/week spread across all weekdays, household 2.1/week. v6 slightly over-serves plain roti (the v6 preamble's own +82 percent specialty-roti flag is measured against the fuller 13-week record; against the 8 as-eaten weeks the specialty rate of 0.7/week sits next to the household's 0.625).

6. **Chicken over-served, never a chicken-free week. IMPROVED.** v5: 23 chicken lunches (2.3/week), minimum 2 every week. v6: 19 chicken lunches (1.9/week), one chicken-free week (week 7) and one single-chicken week. Household: 1.4/week with two zero weeks in 8. Direction right, distance halved, not yet at the household rate.

7. **Carb-only international mains. RESOLVED.** v5: 12 pasta, noodle, or fried-rice lunches, all 12 served solo. v6: 4 such dishes, 3 of them paired with a protein (mushroom risotto with chicken breast, Singapore noodles with paneer tikka, Vietnamese noodle salad with chicken tikka); the unpaired one is paneer fried rice, which carries its own protein. Household: protein added 6 of 8 times.

8. **Egg breakfasts and standalone boiled eggs. IMPROVED.** v5: 14/50 egg breakfasts (28 percent, 1.4/week), standalone boiled eggs 0/50. v6: 18/50 (36 percent, 1.8/week), standalone boiled-eggs-plus-chutney breakfasts 7 times (0.7/week). Household: 17/36 (47 percent, about 2.1/week), standalone 4 times (0.5/week). The standalone form now exists and slightly over-shoots; the overall egg-breakfast rate still runs under the household's.

9. **Paneer at nearly twice the household rate. PERSISTS.** v5: 24 appearances (2.4/week). v6: 23 appearances (2.3/week). Household: 10 in 8 weeks (1.25/week). Essentially unmoved, and the trend inside the run is the wrong way: weeks 1 to 5 carry 8 paneer appearances (1.6/week), weeks 6 to 10 carry 15 (3.0/week), because the exploration affinity score rewards the household's dominant ingredient and each explored paneer dish then enters the record demanding future placements. This looks like a worsening steady behavior, not warm-up.

10. **Dal and legumes at half the household rate. RESOLVED.** v5: 0.7/week, never the lunch main. v6: 16 dal and legume dishes (1.6/week, counting conservatively without moong dal kosambari or chana pulao), with dal or a legume as the lunch's lead dish 7 times. Household: 1.4/week, main 5 times in 8 weeks. v6 now sits marginally over the household rate instead of half of it.

11. **Salads over-served, raita under-served. PERSISTS.** Raita is fixed: v5 0.3/week, v6 0.8/week, household 0.625/week. Salads are not: v5 1.6/week; v6 1.3/week counting vegetable salads only, and 1.8/week counting every salad-named plate (five of them are protein-floor fills: chicken salad, prawn salad, chicken breast salad, paneer salad, egg salad, a shape the household record never contains); household 0.75/week. The vegetable-salad number nudged toward the household, but total salad plates on the table went up, so the finding stands.

12. **Fish tikka under-served and demoted to Saturday. RESOLVED.** v5: 0.5/week, 4 of 5 servings as the fourth item on a Saturday plate. v6: 8 servings (0.8/week), 7 of them on weekdays and 6 of those as the plate's lead protein, 1 on a Saturday. Household: 0.875/week, spread across weekdays as the lead protein.

13. **Overall repetition (the finding underlying 1 to 5). RESOLVED.** v5: 60 lunch slots from 34 distinct lead mains, 57 percent unique, top dishes recurring in the same weekday slot. v6: 60 slots from 48 distinct lead mains, 80 percent unique, 41 of them appearing exactly once, with no dish beyond fish tikka (6, all on different weekdays) exceeding 3. Household: 77 percent unique. v6 also schedules most of the dishes review-1 flagged as eaten-but-never-planned: malai kofta, mutton keema, mutton pepper fry, veg pulao, rajma chawal, vegetable korma, curd rice, mushroom matar. (Dosa and stuffed capsicum remain absent; both were custom one-offs with no library id.)

## Part 2: fresh comparison

### Regressions (v5 was closer to the household than v6 is)

1. **Plate size inflation.** Four-item lunches: household 3/44 (7 percent, 0.375/week); v5 6/60 (10 percent); v6 16/60 (27 percent, 1.6/week). Mean items per lunch: household 2.64, v5 2.50, v6 3.07. v5 erred slightly small, v6 errs decisively large. Every v6 four-item day is the protein floor appending a fourth item to a three-item dal-led or veg-led plate, so this is structural, not warm-up: the count is flat across the run (2, 2, 1, 1, 4, 2, 0, 1, 1, 2 by week).

2. **Non-veg lunch-day rate.** Counting lunch days with any meat, fish, or egg item (review-1's method, which found v5 and the household equal): household 25/44 (0.57), v5 36/60 (0.60), v6 44/60 (0.73). The protein floor converting veg plates into protein plates, plus the exploration channel's egg picks, push v6 well past the household's actual non-veg cadence. Steady, same drivers as regression 1.

3. **Egg-led lunches.** The household ate one egg-led lunch in 8 weeks (egg biryani, 0.125/week). v5 served 3 in 10 weeks (0.3/week). v6 serves 8 (egg pulao, egg curry, Spanish omelette, egg masala dry, shakshuka, egg roast, egg bhurji keto, egg salad; 0.8/week). Exploration's affinity signal loves eggs for the same reason it loves paneer; unlike internationals this will not self-correct with accrual, because each explored egg dish enters the record and earns future placements.

4. **Week-to-week continuity (Jaccard).** Household baseline 0.263; the gate band is 0.20 to 0.35; v5 measured 0.22, inside the band. v6 averages 0.161 (spot-computed week 1 to week 2 at 0.185), below both. Consecutive v6 weeks share little beyond roti, boiled eggs, and fruit; the menu loses the household's texture of comfortable repeats. The preamble attributes this to the warm-up ledger spreading dishes widely, which is plausible, but it is the one gate number that moved away from the household relative to v5 and it needs the 60-week run to confirm the recovery.

5. **International rate (also Part 1 finding 3).** v5 over-served at 2.0/week against the household's 1.4; v6 under-serves at 0.4 to 0.5/week, a larger absolute miss. Warm-up by mechanism (rates of 1/13 need about 13 weeks of accrual), so this regression is the most likely of the five to disappear by the gate window.

6. **Protein salads as the day's protein.** Five protein-floor fills arrive as salads (chicken, prawn, chicken breast, paneer, egg salad). The household's record contains zero protein salads; its floor-style additions were tikkas and grilled preparations. Combined with regression 1 this also lifts total salad plates to 18 vs v5's 16 and the household's 6.

### New behaviors in v6 with no v5 counterpart (mostly toward the household)

- **Mutton surfaces**: 2 servings in 10 weeks (0.2/week) vs the household's 2 in 8; v5 served zero and named this its known soft spot.
- **South Indian surfaces**: curd rice as a Saturday treat, bisi bele bath and moong dal kosambari via exploration, lemon rice as a carb; v5 had none.
- **A Saturday international treat** (Singapore noodles): 1 of 10 Saturdays vs the household's 2 of 8; v5 confined internationals to weekdays.
- **Breakfast pool widens**: 18 distinct breakfast mains vs v5's 14, including poha, sprouts salad, masala oats, anda bhurji, and grilled cheese sandwich; per-occasion breakfast diversity (0.36 distinct per breakfast) now matches the household's exactly. Note three of the newcomers are sandwiches (chicken, paneer, veg) the as-eaten record never shows at breakfast.
- **Single-item breakfasts exist** (poha alone, masala oats alone, bread omelette alone), matching the household's habit; v5 dressed nearly every breakfast with a chutney. v6's chutney rate (0.42 per breakfast) matches the household's 0.42; v5 ran about 0.65.
- **Exploration doubles and diversifies**: exactly 2 picks every week (20 total, all distinct) vs v5's 1 to 2 (11 distinct); picks now include forms v5 never explored (a whole-plate shakshuka lunch, legume salads, pulaos). Cost: 20 new record rows that will each demand future placements, the engine's own preamble flags this.
- **Structural week shapes changed**: the fixed international-Tuesday, exploration-Wednesday skeleton is gone; carbs float (steamed rice and lemon rice appear mid-week); dal-led plates with a protein floor are a recurring lunch form; the Saturday base-plus-special-protein plate (khichdi with paneer do pyaza, veg pulao with mutton pepper fry) reproduces the household's khichdi-with-mutton pattern.

### Warm-up vs steady classification (for the 60-week gate)

- **Warm-up, expect self-correction by weeks 20 to 60**: the international under-serve (accrual arithmetic, about 13 weeks to first eligibility); the low Jaccard (ledger spreading during deficit burn-down); the near-total absence of repeats among one-off mains (80 percent uniqueness should relax toward the band as rates stabilize).
- **Steady, will not fix themselves**: four-item plate inflation and the non-veg day rate (structural protein floor); paneer over-serve (rising within the run, weeks 6 to 10 at 3.0/week, a positive feedback loop through exploration and the record); egg-led lunches (same loop); salad over-serve including protein-salad floor fills; the Winter three-fruit pool (content-bound until §14.4); dessert on 10 of 10 Saturdays vs the household's 6 of 8; avocado toast pinned at exactly 1.0/week vs the household's 0.75; the Thursday boiled-egg lock (household-matching, but in conflict with gate 4 as written).
