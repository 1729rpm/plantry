# Reviewer report: Menu A (proposed 10-week plan) vs Menu B (actual 8 cooked weeks)

Tabulation basis: Menu A has 60 days (10 weeks x 6 days, 50 breakfasts Mon to Fri, 60 lunches, 60 fruit entries). Menu B has 44 days across 8 weeks (W1 missing Friday, W7 missing Monday and Tuesday, W8 missing Monday), giving 36 breakfasts, 44 lunches, 44 fruit entries. All rates normalized to occasions actually present.

## Patterned differences, ranked most systematic first

**1. Menu A runs a rigid day-of-week breakfast template; Menu B has none.**
A locks each weekday breakfast slot: Tuesday avocado toast 10/10 weeks (B: 4/7 Tuesdays, and B also serves it on a Wednesday and a Thursday); Wednesday besan paneer chilla 9/10 (B: 0/8 Wednesdays; B's 3 besan chillas fall on Mon/Tue/Fri); Friday garlic chutney 10/10 and Friday aloo paratha 8/10 (B: garlic chutney on 2/7 Fridays, aloo paratha on 2/7); Monday stuffed paratha 9/10 (B: 1/6 Mondays; B's modal Monday is bread omelette, 3/6). Across 50 planned breakfasts roughly 47 follow the template; B's 36 breakfasts show no repeating day assignment at all.

**2. Menu A collapses to a 3-fruit rotation; Menu B draws on 9 fruits throughout, led by mango.**
In A, banana, papaya, and pomegranate fill 47/60 fruit days (78%), and in weeks 6 to 10 they fill 30/30 consecutive days with the other 6 fruits vanishing entirely. In B those three fruits fill only 17/44 days (39%), and all 9 fruits appear across the 8 weeks. Mango is B's top fruit at 11/44 days (1.4/week) but only 5/60 in A (0.5/week, zero in the last 5 weeks); banana is A's top fruit at 15/60 (25%) but only 4/44 (9%) in B.

**3. Menu A locks international lunches to exactly Tuesday and Friday; Menu B floats them and eats fewer.**
A: 20/20 Tuesday and Friday lunches are international (noodles, pasta, Thai, continental), never on Mon/Wed/Thu/Sat, exactly 2.0/week every week. B: about 11 international lunches in 8 weeks (1.4/week), landing on Mon, Tue, Wed, and twice on Saturday, with a range of 0 per week (W1, W8) to 4 (W6). A both over-serves the category by about 45% per week and fixes it to days B does not keep.

**4. Menu A cycles Saturday from a pool of 4 mains; Menu B's 8 Saturdays have 8 distinct mains.**
A's 10 Saturdays use only chole bhature (3), pav bhaji (3), chicken biryani (2), khichdi (2), in a strict 4-week cycle; its Saturday dessert is a strict 3-item cycle (kheer 4, sewaiyan kheer 3, fruit custard 3). B's 8 Saturday mains are all different (egg biryani, pav bhaji, khichdi with mutton, Thai pineapple fried rice, chole bhature, baked mozzarella pasta, veg pulao, dosa), with 5 distinct desserts including shrikhand and atta halva that A never serves.

**5. Menu A over-serves specialty rotis and under-serves plain roti.**
A serves missi roti 10 times (1.0/week, locked to Wed 8/10) and beetroot roti 10 times (1.0/week, locked to Thu 8/10), versus B's 2 missi (0.25/week) and 3 beetroot (0.375/week) on assorted days. Conversely plain roti appears in 17 of B's 44 lunches (2.1/week) but only 10 of A's 60 (1.0/week, locked to Monday 10/10).

**6. Menu A over-serves chicken and never has a chicken-free week.**
A: 23 chicken lunches in 10 weeks (2.3/week), minimum 2 in every week. B: 11 in 8 weeks (1.4/week), including two full weeks with zero chicken (W4, W8). Overall non-veg rate is the same in both; A shifts the mix toward chicken.

**7. Menu A serves carb-only international mains; Menu B pairs them with a protein.**
A's 12 pasta/noodle/fried-rice lunches (Singapore noodles 3, white sauce pasta 3, hakka 2, baked mozzarella 2, Thai pineapple 2) are served solo 12/12 times. B serves the equivalent dish 8 times and adds a protein 6/8 times (chicken tikka, fish tikka twice, grilled chicken twice, paneer manchurian).

**8. Menu B eats egg breakfasts twice as often, including standalone boiled eggs, which A never serves.**
B: 18/36 breakfasts are egg-based (50%, 2.25/week: 13 boiled-egg entries, 4 bread omelettes, 1 anda paratha), including boiled eggs with chutney as the whole breakfast 4 times. A: 14/50 (28%, 1.4/week), and boiled eggs appear only as a side to sevai/upma/sabudana, never standalone (0/50).

**9. Menu A serves paneer at nearly twice B's rate.**
A: 24 paneer appearances in 10 weeks (2.4/week: 10 besan paneer chillas, 6 paneer parathas, 8 paneer lunch mains). B: 10 in 8 weeks (1.25/week: 3 chillas, 4 parathas, 3 lunch mains).

**10. Menu B eats dal and legumes at twice A's rate.**
B: 11 dal/legume dishes in 8 weeks (1.4/week: moong dal 3, chole 2, toor dal, panchmel dal, dal palak, kadhi, rajma chawal, sambar), including dal as the lunch main 5 times. A: 7 in 10 weeks (0.7/week: moong dal 3, kadhi 2, chole 2), always as a side, never the main.

**11. Menu A over-serves salads; Menu B leans on raita instead.**
A: 16 salads in 10 weeks (1.6/week) plus 3 raitas. B: 6 salads in 8 weeks (0.75/week), 5 of them in the first two weeks and 1 in the last six weeks, plus 5 raitas (0.625/week vs A's 0.3/week).

**12. Fish tikka is under-served in A and demoted to a Saturday add-on.**
B serves fish tikka 7 times in 8 weeks (0.875/week), spread across Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri, Sat, usually as the lunch's lead protein. A serves it 5 times in 10 weeks (0.5/week), 4 of the 5 as the fourth item on a Saturday plate, and only once as a weekday main.

**Overall repetition, underlying findings 1 to 5:** A fills its 60 lunch slots from 34 distinct mains (57% unique, top dishes recurring 3 to 4 times in the same weekday slot); B fills 44 slots from 34 distinct mains (77% unique), with about 24 dishes appearing exactly once, including 10-plus dishes A never schedules at all (malai kofta twice, mutton dishes, dosa, veg pulao, rajma chawal, vegetable korma, curd rice, stuffed capsicum, mushroom matar).

**Deprioritized as too infrequent to call a pattern:** mutton (0 in A vs 2 in B), South Indian mains (0 in A vs 2 in B: dosa-sambar, curd rice), hummus placement (A 3, all Saturday; B 2, both weekday), soya chunks (A 1, B 2).

## Checked and found the same in both menus

- Fruit every single day: A 60/60, B 44/44.
- Dessert confined to Saturday: A 10/10 desserts on Saturday only; B 6/8 Saturdays with dessert, zero weekday desserts in either.
- Saturday is lunch-only (no breakfast): A 10/10, B 8/8.
- Thursday egg-anchored breakfast: A 10/10 (7 boiled-egg sides, 3 bread omelettes), B 7/8; the sevai/upma/sabudana plus boiled eggs combination runs at A 7/10 vs B 5/8, essentially equal.
- Overall non-veg lunch rate: A 36/60 lunch days (0.60) vs B 25/44 (0.57).
- Prawn dishes: A 6 in 10 weeks (0.6/week) vs B 5 in 8 (0.625/week), prawn pepper fry dominant in both.
- Avocado toast overall frequency: A 1.0/week vs B 0.75/week (the day lock, not the amount, is what differs).
- Bhindi as a side: A 3 in 10 weeks vs B 3 in 8 weeks.
- Saturday indulgent one-pot Indian lean: present in both (A 10/10 by construction, B 6/8).
